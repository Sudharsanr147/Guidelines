1. What each planet represents

Each planet is a "significator" (karaka) — it carries certain themes wherever it goes.

Sun (Surya)
Core role Soul, ego, authority, father.
  • Affects: self-confidence, government dealings, status, leadership.
  • Body: heart, eyes, bones, vitality.
  • Strong → respected, principled. Weak → low confidence, ego issues.
Moon (Chandra)
Core role Mind, emotions, mother.
  • Affects: mood, comfort, public popularity, nurturing.
  • Body: chest, fluids, mental health.
  • Strong → calm, intuitive. Weak → anxious, restless mind.
Mars (Mangal/Kuja)
Core role Energy, courage, younger siblings, land.
  • Affects: drive, aggression, accidents, real estate, brothers.
  • Body: blood, muscles, marrow.
  • Strong → bold, decisive. Weak → anger, conflicts, injuries.
Mercury (Budha)
Core role Intellect, speech, communication, commerce.
  • Affects: learning, writing, business, analysis, friends.
  • Body: nerves, skin, voice.
  • Strong → sharp mind, witty. Weak → confused speech, learning issues.
Jupiter (Guru)
Core role Wisdom, dharma, children, husband (for women).
  • Affects: knowledge, guidance, wealth, ethics, religion, teachers.
  • Body: liver, fat, growth.
  • Strong → fortunate, wise. Weak → directionless, faithless.
Venus (Shukra)
Core role Love, marriage, comforts, wife (for men).
  • Affects: relationships, beauty, vehicles, art, luxuries.
  • Body: reproductive system, kidneys, face.
  • Strong → charming, prosperous. Weak → relationship trouble, lack of comfort.
Saturn (Shani)
Core role Discipline, delay, karma, longevity, servants.
  • Affects: hard work, profession, hardship, patience, old age.
  • Body: bones, joints, teeth, nerves.
  • Strong → patient, professional. Weak → delays, depression, struggle.
Rahu (North Node)
Core role Ambition, illusion, foreign things, obsession.
  • Affects: sudden gains, addictions, unconventional paths, fame.
  • Not a physical planet — works through the house and lord it sits with.
  • Strong → out-of-the-box success. Weak → confusion, manipulation, scandals.
Ketu (South Node)
Core role Detachment, spirituality, past-life skills.
  • Affects: moksha, occult, sudden losses, isolation, research.
  • Acts like Mars + a renunciate flavour.
  • Strong → spiritual insight, mastery. Weak → confusion, sudden setbacks.

Quick category check:
Kendra (angles): 1, 4, 7, 10 — pillars of life.
Trikona (trines): 1, 5, 9 — luck and dharma.
Dusthana: 6, 8, 12 — challenges, but also growth.
Upachaya: 3, 6, 10, 11 — improve with age/effort.

3. Planet placement + house lordship = result

Every planet does two jobs at once:

  • Job A — Where it sits (the house it occupies): activates that area of life.
  • Job B — What it owns (the houses where its signs are): carries those houses' affairs with it.
The basic formula:
A planet links its owned house(s) with its occupied house. Result depends on whether those houses are friendly or unfriendly to each other.

Rahu & Ketu don't own signs — they take the flavour of the sign-lord they sit with.

Step 2 — Figure out the house lords for the chart

From the lagna, count houses 1–12. Note the planet that owns the sign on each house cusp. That planet becomes the "lord" of that house.

Example: Lagna is Taurus. Then 1st house = Taurus → Venus is 1st lord. 7th from Taurus is Scorpio → Mars is 7th lord. And so on for all 12 houses.

Step 3 — Read the placement

CombinationPlain-language meaning
Lord of good house (1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11) sits in another good houseBoth areas help each other. Generally favourable.
Lord of good house sits in a bad house (6, 8, 12)That good area suffers — its results get spent, lost or delayed.
Lord of bad house sits in a good houseBrings disturbance to that good area unless well-aspected.
Lord of bad house sits in another bad house"Vipareeta raja yoga" — two negatives can cancel and bring sudden gains.
Lord sits in its own house / own signStrong, stable result for that house's matters.
Lord sits in exaltation (Sun in Aries, Jupiter in Cancer, etc.)Excellent result — high quality of that house's matters.
Lord sits in debilitation (Sun in Libra, Saturn in Aries, etc.)Weak result — that house's matters struggle unless cancellation applies.

Step 4 — Read it as a sentence

Template: "The matters of [house owned] are experienced through [house occupied]."

Example: 7th lord (marriage) sits in 10th house (career) → marriage matters get experienced through career. Could mean: spouse met at work, spouse helps career, career affects marriage timing — exact flavour depends on planet, sign, aspects.
Important checks before finalising:
  • What aspects (drishti) fall on that planet?
  • Is it conjunct another planet? Whose lordship does that planet carry?
  • Is it a natural benefic (Jupiter, Venus, well-placed Moon, Mercury) or natural malefic (Saturn, Mars, Sun, Rahu, Ketu)?
  • Is it a functional benefic for that lagna? (Lords of 1, 5, 9 are usually good; lords of 6, 8, 12 are usually difficult.)

4. How dasha & bhukti deliver the result

The chart shows what can happen. Dasha shows when.

Three layers of timing

  • Mahadasha (MD): the major period — sets the broad theme for those years.
  • Antardasha / Bhukti (AD): sub-period inside the MD — sets the specific theme for those months.
  • Pratyantar (PD): sub-sub-period — fine-tunes timing to weeks.

How to read a dasha–bhukti combination

Result during X-mahadasha & Y-bhukti =
  1. What X represents naturally (its karaka role)
  2. The houses X owns in your chart
  3. The house X occupies
  4. The same three for Y
  5. The relationship between X and Y (friendly? same house? aspecting? in 6/8 from each other?)

Step-by-step method

  1. X's portfolio: karakatva + houses owned + house occupied.
  2. Y's portfolio: same exercise.
  3. Find the link: Does Y sit in a house owned by X? Aspect X? Are they in mutual kendras (1/4/7/10) or trines (1/5/9)? Or in 6/8 (mutually difficult)?
  4. Predict the theme: the period activates the houses common to both X and Y.
  5. Confirm via transit: the actual event triggers when transiting Saturn or Jupiter touches the relevant house/lord.

5. Worked examples

Example 1 — A simple combo
Lagna: Leo. Jupiter is 5th lord (Sagittarius) and 8th lord (Pisces). Suppose Jupiter sits in the 10th house.

Reading: Jupiter carries 5th-house matters (children, intelligence, education) and 8th-house matters (sudden change, in-laws, occult) into the 10th house (career).
→ Career involves teaching/wisdom (Jupiter karaka) + intelligence work (5th) + research or transformation (8th).
→ During Jupiter mahadasha: career rises through teaching, advisory or research-heavy roles. Children-related events possible. Some in-law or sudden-change events too.
Example 2 — Same MD, different bhuktis
Continuing the above, Jupiter MD is running. Now look at bhuktis:

Jupiter MD – Venus AD: Venus is 10th & 3rd lord for Leo. If Venus sits in 11th — strong gains, career boost, relationship/marriage matters.
Jupiter MD – Saturn AD: Saturn is 6th & 7th lord. Mixed — health/work pressure plus marriage tension. Saturn slows; Jupiter expands. Net: hard work bears fruit slowly.
Jupiter MD – Rahu AD: If Rahu is in 10th with Jupiter → sudden career jump, possibly foreign-related. If Rahu is in 12th → expenses, foreign travel, isolation phase.

Same MD theme, but each bhukti delivers a different chapter of it.
Example 3 — Conflict between MD and bhukti lord
Suppose Saturn is your 1st lord (self) and Mars is your 8th lord (sudden events).
In Saturn MD – Mars AD, the 1st lord's period gets overlaid with the 8th lord's bhukti.
→ Pressure on health, body, identity. Hard decisions, possible accidents or sudden lifestyle changes.
→ If Saturn and Mars are in 6/8 from each other in the chart, the difficulty is sharper. If they are in trine (1/5/9), the disruption is constructive — a forced reinvention.
Reading order — five steps:
  1. What the planet is (Section 1).
  2. What house it sits in (Section 2).
  3. What houses it owns in this chart (Section 3, Step 2).
  4. Combine into one sentence (Section 3, Step 4).
  5. Run that sentence through the dasha–bhukti method (Section 4).
That's the whole engine of chart reading.
